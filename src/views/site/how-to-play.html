Player guide

How to compete

Fifteen questions, twelve seconds each, and a reward ladder to ₦50,000. Here's the whole game — on all three platforms.

Step 1

Pick your door

Web, WhatsApp and Telegram all run the same game with one shared account, balance and leaderboard. Start wherever suits you — switch any time.

Web

Fastest to try — nothing to install
  1. Open play.whatsuptrivia.com.ng in any browser
  2. Create your account in under a minute
  3. Tap answers on screen — no typing needed
Open Web-play

WhatsApp

Chat-based — the original way to play
  1. Tap the button below to open our bot
  2. Send "Hi" or "Hello" to start
  3. Register with your name and state
Start on WhatsApp

Telegram

Chat-based — same bot, blue wrapper
  1. Open @WhatsUpTrivia_bot below
  2. Press START or send /start
  3. Register with your name and state
Start on Telegram
Step 2

Choose a competition mode

🎮 Practice Mode

  • Free, unlimited rounds
  • No rewards — purely skill development
  • Available to users 13+

⭐ Classic Mode

  • Uses 1 access credit per round
  • Sponsored rewards up to ₦50,000
  • 12 seconds per question

🏆 Tournaments

  • Sponsored events, enhanced reward pools
  • All three platforms on one leaderboard
  • Watch for announcements
Step 3

Get access credits (for Classic Mode)

In the bot, type BUY; on the web, open the credits screen. One credit enters one round, credits never expire, and payment is encrypted end to end.

Step 4

Play the round

  1. A question appears with 4 options (A, B, C, D)
  2. You have 12 seconds to answer — tap on the web, reply A/B/C/D in chat
  3. Correct? You climb to the next reward tier
  4. Wrong or timeout? The round ends — but any milestone tier you passed is yours to keep
Step 5

Spend your lifelines wisely

50 : 50

Removes two wrong answers, leaving two options. Tap the lifeline on the web, or type "50" in chat. Once per round.

Skip

Swaps the current question for a fresh one at the same difficulty. Tap Skip on the web, or type "SKIP" in chat. Once per round.

💡 Pro tip: save both lifelines for Q11–Q15, where the tiers are steepest.

Step 6

Redeem your reward

  1. Type CLAIM in the bot (or open Claim on the web)
  2. Provide your Nigerian bank details — account name must match your registered name
  3. KYC verification applies to redemptions over ₦10,000
  4. Processing completes within 12–24 hours
⚠️ Rewards must be redeemed within 72 hours of earning them; unredeemed rewards are forfeited.

Tips for the climb

  • Read carefully — 12 seconds is more than it feels
  • Trust your first instinct
  • Hold your lifelines for Q11+
  • Warm up in Practice Mode first
  • Play on a stable connection
