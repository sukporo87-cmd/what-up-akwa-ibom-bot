About What's Up Trivia

Play. Learn. Earn.

Our story

What's Up Trivia was born from a simple idea: make learning fun and rewarding. We believed that knowledge competitions could do more than just entertain — they could educate, challenge, and reward competitors for their skill.

Launched in Nigeria as a WhatsApp bot, the platform has grown into a full multi-platform competition: today the same game engine, question bank, leaderboard and prize pool run on WhatsApp, Telegram and a full browser game at play.whatsuptrivia.com.ng — with thousands of trivia enthusiasts testing their knowledge daily on a fair, skill-based platform.

Our mission

To create the most engaging, fair, and rewarding skill-based knowledge competition in Nigeria — where knowledge truly pays off, on whatever platform you already use.

What we do

  • 🎮 Deliver exciting knowledge competitions on WhatsApp, Telegram and the web
  • 💰 Recognize knowledge with sponsored leaderboard rewards up to ₦50,000
  • ⚖️ Provide a fair, skill-based competition experience
  • 🔒 Maintain the highest standards of security and integrity
  • 🌍 Create an inclusive platform for all knowledge enthusiasts

Our values

Fairness

Every competitor has an equal opportunity. Our questions are carefully curated and randomly selected — and every platform plays by exactly the same rules.

Transparency

Clear rules, visible reward tier structures, live public leaderboards, and honest communication.

Security

Your data, payments, and competition rounds are protected by industry-leading measures.

Responsibility

We promote responsible play and provide tools to maintain healthy habits — including caps, cooldowns and self-exclusion.

  • ✅ Responsive customer support

For partners & sponsors

What's Up Trivia is more than a consumer game — it's a messaging-native engagement engine. Tournaments run simultaneously across WhatsApp, Telegram and the web on one shared leaderboard, which makes the platform a natural home for sponsored competitions, branded question sets, and audience activations. If your organisation is interested in sponsoring a tournament or building on the platform, we'd like to hear from you at hello@whatsuptrivia.com.ng.
