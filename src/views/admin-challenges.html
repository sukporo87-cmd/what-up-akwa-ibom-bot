⚔️ Challenges

Invite funnel

Held challenges are excluded from every figure on this page. A challenge where anti-collusion tripped is not a real accept and not a real completion.

Mode, format and completion

Completion rate counts only settled challenges. One still inside its 48-hour invite window has not failed yet, and counting it as a failure makes every fresh day look like a collapse.

Group size — actual players, not the cap chosen

Sponsored prizes

Sponsored prizes are pass-through money, not house cost. The sponsor pays it in and we hand it on, so gross in and paid out are reported as a matched pair. The only figure the business actually keeps is the 15% retained on challenges that did not complete.

Challenges

Rematches are hidden by default. They are generated by a result card rather than created by a person, and counting them made the funnel report more challenges created than invites sent.

Modelled live-arena bandwidth

Modelled, not measured — compare against Render's own metrics. If actual runs materially above this, the first thing to check is whether anything is emitting per-answer.
