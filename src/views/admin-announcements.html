📣 News Bar

The scrolling bar that sits under the header on every page of the website. Notices appear within about a minute of saving.

End time is the field worth using. Set one and the notice takes itself down — nothing stays on the site because somebody forgot it was there. Leave both times empty and it runs until you hide it. Higher priority scrolls first.

Plain text only: any HTML typed here appears on the site literally, as characters. Hide takes a notice off the site but keeps the text; delete is permanent.
