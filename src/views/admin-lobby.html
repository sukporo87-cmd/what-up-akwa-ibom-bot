📋 Lobby Board

The board Tiva holds while players wait for a live challenge to start. They sit here for up to five minutes, so it is the one screen where you have their full attention. Slides rotate in order and repeat until the match begins. Changes appear within about a minute.

Text is drawn on white, like a real whiteboard. Image and video fill the board; both must be https addresses, and video plays muted with no sound.

End time is the field worth using. Set one and the slide takes itself down. Higher priority shows first. Hide takes a slide off the board but keeps it; delete is permanent.
